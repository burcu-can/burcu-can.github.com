Short biography

I am a Senior Lecturer in Computing Science at the Department of Computing Science and Mathematics, University of Stirling in Scotland as a member of Data Science and Intelligent Systems Research Group.

Research interests

I work on problems in Natural Language Processing and Computational Linguistics, focusing on learning in low-resource settings, including resource-scarce languages and child language acquisition. My research interests include linguistic aspects of language, such as morphology and syntax, as well as practical applications, including domain adaptation of language models for real-world tasks.
